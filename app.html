About LedgerLoop

Application information and build details.

  • Version 1.2.0 (Local Build)
  • Project Personal Finance MMEL04
  • Storage Encrypted Local Storage

1. Application Workflow

Follow this sequence during initial setup, and repeat weekly to maintain accurate records.

  • Data Entry: Navigate through each of the 5 Pillar tabs (Income, Spending, Savings, Investment, Protection). Utilize the provided dropdowns and date pickers to ensure standardized entries.
  • Dashboard Review: Return to the Dashboard to analyze your automated summary cards and charts. Cross-reference these metrics to ensure they match your pillar inputs.
  • Period Analysis: Utilize the Weekly/Monthly report tab to monitor income against spending over specific timeframes, enabling early identification of financial trends.

2. Reports & Data Export

Professional tools for presenting and documenting your financial records.

  • Privacy-First Architecture: This application securely stores your data locally within your browser session. There are no external databases.
  • Print Summary of Transactions: Use the built-in print feature in your reports view to generate clean, physical or PDF documentation of your financial activity for client reviews or grading.
  • Export to Excel (.csv): Download your complete ledger data into a structured spreadsheet format for advanced external analysis and custom reporting.
  • Clear All: Use this strictly to wipe the current session state for a clean presentation run-through.

The 5 Financial Pillars

The project rubric requires all five pillars to be complete and connected. Here is the operational definition for each.

1. Income

Every unit of incoming capital.

Includes: Salary, business revenues, freelance income, gifts, and side hustles.

Action: Log the date received and source.

2. Spending

Every unit of outgoing capital.

Includes: Food, transport, housing, and bills.

Action: Establish monthly categorical budgets and track actual expenditures against them.

3. Savings & Goals

Capital allocated for specific objectives.

Includes: Emergency funds, tuition, or gadget funds.

Action: Define target amounts, deadlines, and log deposits to track progress.

4. Investment

Capital deployed to generate returns.

Includes: Stocks, mutual funds, crypto, time deposits.

Action: Input invested capital vs. current value; ROI is calculated automatically.

5. Protection

Risk management and coverage.

Includes: Life, health, property, and vehicle insurance.

Action: Track coverage amounts, monthly premiums, and renewal dates.

Metric Calculations Reference

How each figure on the main dashboard is mathematically derived.

Dashboard Figure Formula / Logic
Total Income (Month) Sum of all Income entries dated within the current calendar month.
Total Spending (Month) Sum of all Expense entries dated within the current calendar month.
Net Cash Flow Total Income (Month) - Total Spending (Month)
Savings Rate (Net Cash Flow ÷ Total Income) × 100
Total Saved Cumulative sum of the "current amount" across every active savings goal.
Investment Value Cumulative sum of the "current value" across every active investment asset.
Protection Coverage Cumulative sum of the "coverage amount" across every active protection plan.
Net Worth Trend Running historical total of (Savings + Investment Value) as of each period.

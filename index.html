The 5 Pillars of Wealth

We built LedgerLoop around the foundational principles of personal finance. Everything has its place.

1. Income

Log salaries, freelance gigs, and side hustles. See exactly how much capital you are generating month over month.

2. Spending

Set categories, build monthly budgets, and track your outflows to ensure you always live below your means.

3. Savings

Create specific goals (like an Emergency Fund or Vacation) and track targeted deposits until you hit your deadline.

4. Investments

Track your portfolio across stocks, mutual funds, and crypto. Monitor your invested capital versus current market value.

5. Protection

A true financial plan expects the unexpected. Log your life, health, and property insurance coverages in one secure view.

How it works

A simple, secure workflow designed to build intentional financial habits.

01

Set your baseline

Establish your monthly budget limits across categories and create your initial savings goals. Define what success looks like for the month before you spend a peso.

02

Log intentionally

Manually enter your daily transactions. We believe the deliberate act of tracking builds significantly better financial awareness than passive, automated bank syncing.

03

Monitor the loop

Watch your unified dashboard update in real-time. Spot spending trends, check your net cash flow, and ensure your savings rate is on track to hit your targets.

About LedgerLoop

Most modern finance apps demand your bank login credentials, pull your transaction history to their servers, and bombard you with credit card offers. We believe your financial data belongs strictly to you.

LedgerLoop was built as a privacy-first alternative. Everything you enter is saved locally, right in your own browser storage. There are no external databases, no hidden tracking analytics, and no bank connections. It is a pure, fast, and entirely private ledger designed to give you clarity across all five pillars of your financial life.

LedgerLoop is designed to provide a structured and accessible platform for personal financial management. It aims to help users organize and monitor their financial activities, improve their understanding of their financial position, and support informed and responsible financial decision-making.
